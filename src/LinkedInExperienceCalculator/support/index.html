Privacy Policy

  • 🏠 Local Processing: All data extraction and calculation happens locally on your own computer, within your browser.
  • 🚫 No External Servers: We do not send your data, or the LinkedIn data of the profiles you view, to any external servers.
  • 🔑 Permissions: We only request the permissions strictly necessary to read the HTML of the LinkedIn tab you are viewing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does it get my data?

It doesn't! The extension simply reads the public HTML of the LinkedIn profile you are currently looking at and calculates the dates using regular expressions (Regex).

Will LinkedIn ban me for using this?

No. The extension does not perform automated scraping across multiple pages or spam the LinkedIn servers. It just reads the HTML of the page you have already loaded in your active tab.
