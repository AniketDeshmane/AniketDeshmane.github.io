Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we handle your data.

Data Collection and Usage

  • Local Processing: All data extraction and calculation happens locally on your own computer, within your browser.
  • No External Servers: We do not send your data, or the LinkedIn data of the profiles you view, to any external servers.
  • Permissions: We only request the permissions strictly necessary to read the DOM and fetch experience data from the LinkedIn tab you are currently viewing.
